Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version
  • We collect only what's needed to run your account and the course: your email, your progress, and anything you choose to submit, like a review.
  • Stripe handles payments. We never see or store your card number.
  • We keep a security log (including IP addresses) to protect accounts and payments, for 90 days.
  • We don't sell your data, run ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • Email us any time to see or delete your data.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the course: logging you in, unlocking content you paid for, and saving your progress.
  • To handle payments and refunds.
  • To answer support requests and send messages about your account or purchase.
  • To improve the course, using reviews and feedback.
  • To keep accounts, payments and paid content secure, using the security log.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. Data stored on your device

PlutoScan uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies. It holds:

  • Your login session (managed by Firebase), so you stay signed in.
  • A copy of your course progress, so the course loads quickly.
  • Your watchlist, Research Scanner answers and any projects you add manually. These stay on that device only and aren't sent to us.

Clearing your browser's site data removes these. Your course progress is restored from your account the next time you log in.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your login, progress and review from our systems.

Security log entries are deleted automatically after 90 days. Security alerts raised from them (for example, many failed sign-ins from one IP address) are kept for up to one year. Stripe keeps its own payment records as required by financial regulations.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Send you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your account and data. Deleting your account ends your course access.
  • Stop featuring a review you allowed us to publish.

Email support@plutoscan.com from the address on your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. Course content is only released by our server after it verifies your login and purchase. All connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there's less to protect.

8. Age requirement

PlutoScan is for adults aged 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also let account holders know by email.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: support@plutoscan.com
