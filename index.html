What it checks

Security & Stability

5 checks · counts the most
  • Market cap over $1B
  • 24h trading volume over $50M
  • Max supply known and capped
  • Listed on more than 5 exchanges
  • FDV within 3× of market cap

Fundamentals

5 checks
  • Clear, real use case YOU
  • Active open-source development
  • Roadmap with delivered milestones YOU
  • Identifiable, credible team YOU
  • Ranked in the top 250 by market cap

Community & Momentum

2 checks · counts the least
  • Active community
  • 30-day price move under 40%

Checks marked YOU are your judgment calls. Every other check fills itself in from live data.
